Method pipeline

Part-based RePaint for colored point-cloud completion

Geometry is completed by a frozen PoinTr; the open question is how to color the points it invents. The previous rule painted each filled point with its part's mean visible color — but a mean is flat, and a part that is fully occluded has nothing to average. This pipeline replaces that rule with RePaint (Lugmayr et al., CVPR 2022): a diffusion model over the per-point color field, trained unconditionally, conditioned on part, with the known colors re-injected at every reverse step.

3 · How RePaint fills the hole

xt-1 = m ⊙ q(xknown, t-1) + (1 − m) ⊙ pθ(xt)

At every reverse step the known colors are forward-diffused to the current noise level and written back over the mask. The model itself is trained unconditionally and never sees an occlusion mask — so one model covers 25 / 50 / 75 % occlusion with no retraining. The schedule also jumps back up in time (resampling), which is what lets the generated region harmonize with the known one rather than merely match its texture.

What makes it part-based

  • Conditioned denoiser — part one-hot plus within-part feature pooling, so a part's visible colors drive its own missing points. The learned form of the part-mean rule.
  • Per-part anchor accounting — a part with zero visible points is flagged anchorless and filled from the prior instead of bleeding a neighbour's color.
  • Visibility-adaptive resampling — the time-travel budget follows the worst-anchored part, spending compute where the conditioning is weak.

4 · Why real texture is the experiment that counts

On synthetic color the ceiling is 5.5 and part-mean already sits on it — nothing can win there, so Stage 0 only proves the machinery runs. On real texture the ceiling rises to 20.7: part-mean is structurally stuck at ~20 while a generative model is not. That gap is the entire opportunity.

The variation is not a smooth gradient — median 13 unique colors per 8192 points. It is several flat materials inside one semantic part (white fuselage, dark stripe). That favours sampling over averaging: the mean of two materials is a tone the object never wears.

5 · Evaluation

Mean ΔE(Lab) between completion and ground truth, measured only on the missing region, on held-out models. Compared rows: NN-copy (nearest visible point) · part-mean (previous method) · RePaint-vanilla (part-blind ablation) · RePaint-part · RePaint-part with oracle segmentation · oracle ceiling. The last two separate the two error sources: the gap between them is segmentation error alone, not a limit of the coloring idea.

Caveat that belongs in the paper. ΔE is a distortion metric, so it provably rewards the conditional mean — the quantity a regressor or a part-mean already outputs. A generative model is structurally disadvantaged on it (distortion–perception trade-off, Blau & Michaeli, 2018). Report distribution metrics alongside: best-of-k ΔE, inter-sample variance, per-part color-histogram distance.

Geometry stays frozen throughout — the contribution is in the color, not the shape. PoinTr's frame is resolved by search rather than by eye: Chamfer to ground truth drops from 0.1622 (raw partial, no completion) to 0.0616 once the correct signed permutation is used — a 2.6× improvement, while the wrong frame roughly doubles the error. The categorical palette on this page is validated for colour-vision deficiency across all pairs in both light and dark modes.
