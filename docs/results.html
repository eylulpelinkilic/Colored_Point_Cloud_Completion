Benchmark results · 13–20 August 2026

Evidence review

Twenty-seven measured cells across two method arms and five datasets, read against what each one can actually establish. Several conclusions carried through the week do not survive the full table; they are corrected here rather than quietly dropped.

arms: RePaint-part · joint-6D datasets: ours · 3DCoMPaT200 · DensePoint · PartNeXt source: W&B colored-pc-completion

1 · The ledger

Across all 27 cells of the RePaint arm, counting how often each method beats the NN-copy baseline — copy the colour of the nearest visible point:

RePaint-vanilla (part-blind)
0 / 27

Never wins. Diffusion alone, without part information, loses in every single cell.

part-mean (oracle labels)
12 / 27

A flat per-part average beats the baseline in nearly half the cells — the part label carries real information.

RePaint-part (oracle labels)
5 / 27

All five sit in the two configurations where the part-constant ceiling is far below NN-copy. That is not a coincidence — see §5.

RePaint-part (trained segmenter)
3 / 27

Two clear wins, both ours/airplane/long4k. The third is a 0.10 dE margin and should not be counted.

The one clean result

Part-blind diffusion never beats the trivial baseline — 0 out of 27. Adding part conditioning moves the same architecture to 5 wins with accurate labels. Whatever else is uncertain, the gain in this system comes from part awareness, not from diffusion.

5 · The ceiling predicts what is possible

The oracle ceiling paints every point its own part's true mean colour. No part-constant method can score below it. Comparing that ceiling to NN-copy says, before any model is trained, whether part information can help at all in a given cell.

margin = oracle ceiling − NN-copy (negative = parts carry usable signal)
margincells oracle-seg beats NNmean oracle-seg result
ceiling more than 8 dE below NN33 / 3−7.14 dE
between 2 and 8 dE below132 / 13+8.69 dE
less than 2 dE below, or above110 / 11+18.80 dE

Monotone, and the correlation across all 27 cells is r = 0.61. The rule is one-directional and that is how it should be used: a large margin does not promise a win, but a small one rules it out. Where the ceiling cannot beat NN-copy, nothing built on part-constant colour can.

6 · Segmentation cost tracks vocabulary size

The gap between RePaint-part and RePaint-part (oracle seg) is what the trained segmenter costs. It is negligible everywhere except one place:

dataset / categoryseg acc part vocabularycostreading
3DCoMPaT200 / chair57.1 %22+8.37 dEThe bottleneck. Oracle labels win by 6.6; the segmenter turns that into a 1.9 loss
PartNeXt / Chair73.8 %7−1.52 dETrained labels marginally better than oracle — within noise
ours / airplane long4k85.0 %4+0.51 dESegmentation is not the limit here
DensePoint / chair85.5 %4+0.98 dEAlso not the limit — the ceiling is

This separates two failure modes that look identical in the headline number. On DensePoint the method fails because the data has no part-colour signal. On 3DCoMPaT200 the signal is the strongest we have measured and the method fails because it cannot find the parts. Only the second is fixable by better modelling.

7 · The joint-6D arm

Geometry and colour from one diffusion model, no frozen PoinTr. Nine cells, complete.

  • Colour loses in 9 / 9, by +4.06 ± 0.94 dE against NN-copy evaluated on the same generated geometry. Low variance across category and difficulty — systematic, not noise.
  • Geometry is competitive. Chamfer 0.044 at simple against frozen PoinTr's 0.054; PoinTr pulls ahead at hard (0.073 vs 0.089).
  • Completion only pays off under heavy occlusion. The ratio to returning the input unchanged is 0.98–1.15 at simple, rising to 2.4–3.9 at hard. Both arms show this.
Verdict withheld

The ablation separating the single-sample penalty and the correspondence confound has not been run. Until it is, +4.06 cannot be attributed to the model rather than to the measurement — ΔE rewards the conditional mean, and a sampler pays variance for it. The notebook is ready and takes about an hour.

8 · Corrections

carried this weekcorrected
“RePaint-part loses by +9.61 ± 3.04” That is the base/hard cell — unusable configuration. The valid figure is +2.99, and at simple and moderate the method wins
“Part conditioning wins” Over-claimed from three airplane rows. It wins in 2 of 27 cells with a trained segmenter, 5 with oracle labels. What holds without qualification is the negative: part-blind diffusion wins 0 of 27
“The fixes flipped the result” The training budget did most of the work: 300 → 4000 epochs, alongside the frame and density fixes
DensePoint has “no headroom”, so drop it Right conclusion, and now confirmed on the real run — its ceiling is 0.37 dE worse than NN-copy. But it was dismissed too early on a comparison with our own car, which is equally thin
headroom.py measures usable signal It compares the oracle to a global mean, a much weaker baseline than NN-copy, so it overstates. Our chair reads 3.85 there but its real margin is −0.08. The script needs an NN-copy comparator

9 · What is established, and what is not

Established

  • Part-blind diffusion does not beat nearest-neighbour copying — 0 of 27 cells, across five datasets.
  • With accurate part labels and sufficient training, part conditioning beats it, and can exceed the part-constant ceiling: 14.90 against a ceiling of 15.37 on ours/airplane/simple.
  • The ceiling-to-NN margin, measurable before training, rules out cells where no part method can win.
  • DensePoint and PartNeXt chair carry no usable part-colour signal. This is a property of those datasets and is reportable as such.
  • Segmentation is the binding constraint exactly once — 3DCoMPaT200, 22 parts, 57.1 % accuracy, 8.37 dE cost.

Not established

  • Whether the win generalises beyond airplane. Car and chair have never been run in a valid configuration.
  • Whether the joint-6D loss is real or a measurement artefact. The ablation is unrun.
  • Whether 3DCoMPaT200 confirms the claim. Its oracle rows are promising, but the trained result is at 300 epochs.
  • Anything about statistical significance. Cells are means over 15 test models, one seed, no test.
